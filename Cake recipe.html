How to Bake a Vanilla Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ cups (300g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 ½ teaspoons baking powder
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup (170g) unsalted butter (softened to room temperature)
  • 1 ¾ cups granulated sugar
  • 3 large eggs (room temperature)
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 1 ¼ cups milk or buttermilk (room temperature)

  • Instructions

    1. Prep the Oven and Pans

  • 2. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  • 3. Grease two 8-inch round cake pans with baking spray or butter, and line the bottoms with parchment paper rounds.
  • 4. Mix the Dry Ingredients
  • 5. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt until well combined.
  • 6. Cream the Butter and Sugar
  • 7. In a large bowl (or stand mixer), beat the softened butter on medium speed for about 30 to 40 seconds until smooth.
  • 8. Add the granulated sugar and mix on high for 2 to 3 minutes until light and fluffy.
  • 9. Add Eggs and Vanilla
  • 10. Beat in the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
  • 11. Pour in the vanilla extract and mix on high for about 1 minute until fluffy.
  • 12. Alternate Dry Ingredients and Milk
  • 13. On low speed, add a portion of the flour mixture, then a splash of milk (or buttermilk), alternating between the two and ending with the flour mixture.
  • 14. Mix just until combined—take care not to overmix the batter. 15. Bake and Cool 16. Divide the batter evenly between your prepared cake pans and smooth the tops with a spatula. 17. Bake at 350°F for 25 to 30 minutes, or until a toothpick or skewer inserted into the center comes out clean. 18. Let the cakes cool in their pans for 10 to 15 minutes, then turn them out onto a wire rack to cool completely before frosting

    How to Make Buttercream icing

    Ingredients

    • 1.1 cup (227g) unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
    • 2.4 cups (480g) powdered sugar (icing sugar)
    • 3.¼ cup (60ml) heavy cream or whole milk
    • 4.2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
    • 5.¼ teaspoon salt (to balance the sweetness)

    Instructions

    1. 1. Whip the butter: Beat the softened butter in a large bowl with a hand mixer or stand mixer on medium-high speed for 2 to 3 minutes until completely smooth and creamy.
    3. 2. Add sugar gradually: Turn the mixer to low and slowly add the powdered sugar one cup at a time, mixing until incorporated so it doesn't fly out of the bowl.
    5. 3. Add liquids and salt: Pour in the heavy cream (or milk), vanilla extract, and salt.
    7. 4. Whip until fluffy: Increase the speed to medium-high and beat for another 3 to 4 minutes until the frosting is light, airy, and spreadable.
    9. 5. Adjust consistency: If it is too thick, add a teaspoon of cream at a time; if it is too thin, beat in a bit more powdered sugar.

      10. 5 Essential Cake Baking Tips

        1. 1.Use room-temperature ingredients: Let your butter, eggs, and milk sit out for 30–60 minutes before baking so they blend smoothly without curdling.
        3. 2. Measure flour accurately: Spoon flour into your measuring cup and level it off with a knife rather than scooping directly from the bag, which packs in too much flour and makes the cake dry.
        5. 3. Avoid overmixing the batter: Once you add the flour, mix just until the white streaks disappear; overmixing develops gluten and makes the cake dense or tough.
        7. 4.Keep the oven door closed: Avoid opening the oven during the first 20 minutes of baking, as sudden drops in temperature can cause the center of your cake to sink.
        9. 5.Cool completely before frosting: Wait until the cake layers are totally cool to the touch; frosting a warm cake will melt the buttercream and make it slide right off.
