How to Bake a Vanilla Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ cups (300g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 ½ teaspoons baking powder
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup (170g) unsalted butter (softened to room temperature)
  • 1 ¾ cups granulated sugar
  • 3 large eggs (room temperature)
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 1 ¼ cups milk or buttermilk (room temperature)

  • Instructions

    1. Prep the Oven and Pans • Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). • Grease two 8-inch round cake pans with baking spray or butter, and line the bottoms with parchment paper rounds. 2. Mix the Dry Ingredients • In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt until well combined. 3. Cream the Butter and Sugar • In a large bowl (or stand mixer), beat the softened butter on medium speed for about 30 to 40 seconds until smooth. • Add the granulated sugar and mix on high for 2 to 3 minutes until light and fluffy. 4. Add Eggs and Vanilla • Beat in the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition. • Pour in the vanilla extract and mix on high for about 1 minute until fluffy. 5. Alternate Dry Ingredients and Milk • On low speed, add a portion of the flour mixture, then a splash of milk (or buttermilk), alternating between the two and ending with the flour mixture. • Mix just until combined—take care not to overmix the batter. 6. Bake and Cool • Divide the batter evenly between your prepared cake pans and smooth the tops with a spatula. • Bake at 350°F for 25 to 30 minutes, or until a toothpick or skewer inserted into the center comes out clean. • Let the cakes cool in their pans for 10 to 15 minutes, then turn them out onto a wire rack to cool completely before frosting
