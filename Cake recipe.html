How to Bake a Vanilla Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ cups (300g) all-purpose flour.
  • 2 ½ teaspoons baking powder.
  • ¼ teaspoon salt.
  • ¾ cup (170g) unsalted butter (softened to room temperature).
  • 1 ¾ cups granulated sugar.
  • 3 large eggs (room temperature).
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract.
  • 1 ¼ cups milk or buttermilk (room temperature).

Instructions

    Prep the Oven and Pans.
  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Grease two 8-inch round cake pans with baking spray or butter, and line the bottoms with parchment paper rounds.
    3. Mix the Dry Ingredients.
  3. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt until well combined.
    4. Cream the Butter and Sugar. In a large bowl (or stand mixer), beat the softened butter on medium speed for about 30 to 40 seconds until smooth. Add the granulated sugar and mix on high for 2 to 3 minutes until light and fluffy. Add Eggs and Vanilla. Beat in the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition. Pour in the vanilla extract and mix on high for about 1 minute until fluffy. Alternate Dry Ingredients and Milk. On low speed, add a portion of the flour mixture, then a splash of milk (or buttermilk), alternating between the two and ending with the flour mixture.
  4. Mix just until combined—take care not to overmix the batter.
    5. Bake and Cool. Divide the batter evenly between your prepared cake pans and smooth the tops with a spatula. Bake at 350°F for 25 to 30 minutes, or until a toothpick or skewer inserted into the center comes out clean. Let the cakes cool in their pans for 10 to 15 minutes, then turn them out onto a wire rack to cool completely before frosting.

How to Make Buttercream icing

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (227g) unsalted butter, softened to room temperature.
  • 4 cups (480g) powdered sugar (icing sugar).
  • ¼ cup (60ml) heavy cream or whole milk.
  • 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract.
  • ¼ teaspoon salt (to balance the sweetness).

Instructions

  1. Whip the butter: Beat the softened butter in a large bowl with a hand mixer or stand mixer on medium-high speed for 2 to 3 minutes until completely smooth and creamy.
  2. Add sugar gradually: Turn the mixer to low and slowly add the powdered sugar one cup at a time, mixing until incorporated so it doesn't fly out of the bowl.
  3. Add liquids and salt: Pour in the heavy cream (or milk), vanilla extract, and salt.
  4. Whip until fluffy: Increase the speed to medium-high and beat for another 3 to 4 minutes until the frosting is light, airy, and spreadable.
  5. Adjust consistency: If it is too thick, add a teaspoon of cream at a time; if it is too thin, beat in a bit more powdered sugar.

      6. 5 Baking tips

      1. Use room-temperature ingredients: Let your butter, eggs, and milk sit out for 30–60 minutes before baking so they blend smoothly without curdling.
      2. Measure flour accurately: Spoon flour into your measuring cup and level it off with a knife rather than scooping directly from the bag, which packs in too much flour and makes the cake dry.
      3. Avoid overmixing the batter: Once you add the flour, mix just until the white streaks disappear; overmixing develops gluten and makes the cake dense or tough.
      4. Keep the oven door closed: Avoid opening the oven during the first 20 minutes of baking, as sudden drops in temperature can cause the center of your cake to sink.
      5. Cool completely before frosting: Wait until the cake layers are totally cool to the touch; frosting a warm cake will melt the buttercream and make it slide right off.
