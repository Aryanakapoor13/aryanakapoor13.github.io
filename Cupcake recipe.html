Vanilla Cupcakes

Ingredients

  1. 1 and 3/4 cups (207g) cake flour (spooned and leveled)
  2. 3/4 teaspoon baking powder
  3. 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
  4. 1/4 teaspoon salt
  5. 1/2 cup (8 Tbsp; 113g) unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
  6. 1 cup (200g) granulated sugar
  7. 3 large egg whites, at room temperature
  8. 1/2 cup (120g) full-fat sour cream at room temperature
  9. 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
  10. seeds scraped from 1/2 of a vanilla bean (optional)
  11. 1/2 cup (120g/ml) whole milk at room temperature
  12. vanilla buttercream
  13. sprinkles, for garnish (optional)

Instructions.

  • Step 1: Prep Oven and Pans Preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C). Line a 12-cup muffin pan with cupcake liners, and line a second pan with 2 liners for about 14 cupcakes total. Set aside.
  • Step 2: Mix Dry Ingredients Whisk the cake flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together in a medium bowl. Set aside.
  • Step 3: Cream Butter and Sugar Beat the butter and sugar on high speed in a large bowl using a handheld or stand mixer with a paddle attachment for about 3 minutes until light and creamy. Scrape down the sides of the bowl as needed.
  • Step 4: Add Egg Whites and Vanilla Add the egg whites, vanilla extract, and vanilla bean seeds (if using). Beat on high speed for about 2 minutes until smooth and well combined.
  • Step 5: Mix in Sour Cream Add the sour cream and beat on medium-high speed for about 1 minute until combined.
  • Step 6: Add Dry Ingredients and Milk Run the mixer on low speed and add the dry ingredients, then slowly pour in the milk. Beat just until combined without over-mixing. Whisk by hand a few times if needed to remove any lumps at the bottom of the bowl. The batter will be slightly thick.
  • Step 7: Fill Liners and Bake Spoon or pour the batter into the liners about 2/3 full to prevent spilling. Bake for 19–22 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  • Step 8: Cool Cupcakes Cool the cupcakes in the pan on a cooling rack for 20 minutes. Remove them from the pan and return them to the rack to cool completely before frosting.
  • Step 9: Frost and Store Frost the cooled cupcakes with vanilla buttercream using an icing knife or a piping tip like a Wilton 1M. Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.

    • Tips

    1. Gather your tools: 12-count muffin pan, cupcake liners, glass mixing bowl, whisk, handheld or stand electric mixer, icing knife, piping bag (reusable or disposable), Wilton 1M piping tip, and a cupcake carrier.
    2. Bring all refrigerated ingredients to room temperature so the batter mixes evenly.
    3. Use cake flour for the lightest texture (homemade works in a pinch, though it won't be as light).
    4. Prep your vanilla: pure vanilla extract is best; if using imitation, use 1 tablespoon (3 teaspoons), or use 2 teaspoons of vanilla bean paste instead of extract and bean.
    5. Prep your dairy: use whole milk and sour cream for best taste and texture, or swap in full-fat plain yogurt, lower-fat milk, nondairy milk, or 1 cup (240 g/ml) buttermilk.
